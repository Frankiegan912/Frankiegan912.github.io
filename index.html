About Me

I am a master's student at Waseda University, advised by Prof. Shigeru Shimamoto. I study learning and decision-making in multi-agent and networked systems.

My current work examines decentralized wireless control under shared constraints and how to test whether adaptive mechanisms use the feedback they are designed to use. I am also interested in reliable evaluation for autonomous research agents. Across these projects, I use problem structure, controlled interventions, and independent audits to distinguish apparent performance from behavior that holds under evaluation.

Research & Experience

Causally Verified QoS-Floor Constraints for Decentralized SAGIN Access Association

Waseda University · 2026 · first author · manuscript under review at IEEE CCNC 2027

I formulated decentralized access association as an interference-coupled constrained Markov game and implemented MAPPO baselines in EPyMARL. Controlled cut, cross-agent misdirection, and sign-reversal interventions test whether learned multipliers carry the intended constraint signal. In the reported simulations, the revised expected-shortfall constraint raised the per-slot floor by 17.1× at N = 12 and 63.1× at N = 20 over matched unconstrained MAPPO; the study remains simulation-only.

ShinsaEvolve: Independent Audits in LLM-Driven Recipe Evolution

Independent research project and technical report · 2026 · not peer reviewed

I developed an audit-gated program-evolution system that evaluates trained policies on fresh seeds using harness-controlled raw rewards without executing candidate recipe code during audits. A 2×2 LunarLander study covered 12 independent outer-loop runs and 180 candidates. Candidate-shaped rewards amplified selection errors; fixed-raw training removed score saturation but did not eliminate training-to-audit misranking. Code and reproducibility artifacts · Technical report

Bio-inspired Mapping and Navigation for Robots

University of Cincinnati · 2024–2025 · supervised by Prof. Ali Minai

I contributed to a hippocampus-inspired navigation model using place-cell, boundary-vector-cell, and head-direction-cell representations. My work included visual preprocessing, CNN feature extraction, multimodal fusion experiments, parameter tuning, and analysis of representation stability in simulation.

RGB-Thermal Semantic Segmentation for Autonomous Driving

SERES Group · 2025 · AI algorithm engineering internship

I adapted DFormerV2 for RGB-thermal inputs, implemented multimodal fusion modules, and built the training and evaluation workflow for comparative and ablation studies.
